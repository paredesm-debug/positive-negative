Positive Shapes & Negative Spaces

Look closely, make choices, and test your understanding. In this activity, you will sort, identify, and match the occupied shapes and open spaces that artists use to build a composition.

Remember: A positive shape is the subject or form. Negative space is the open area around, between, or inside the subject. Both are important parts of an artwork.
